YACEWO — Yet Another Chess Enigma, Written in OCaml

Two-player chess in your browser. Play hotseat on a single phone, or share a room link and play someone remotely. No account, no install.

Four variants: Classical, Anarchy, Chess960 and Horde. Paste a FEN to pick up any position, click pieces or type moves like e4, Nf3, O-O, and switch between light and dark themes.

The board needs JavaScript. Source and a terminal version live on GitHub.
